About

A love story, three boys, and one taco worth building a brand around.

Travana Tacos is a Rochester pop-up with Mexican flavor roots and a very focused promise: one taco done right.

Point of view

Minimalism is not the backup plan. It is the whole power move.

A tiny menu can feel braver than a big one. Travana is designed around the idea that people remember the best thing you make, not the longest menu you print.

That means fewer items, cleaner service, and more attention on the quesabirria box that has to carry the brand.

How Travana got here

Built from family, flavor, and restraint.

Travana is personal: Rochester pride, Mexican flavor roots, and a family-built brand trying to make one thing feel special instead of making everything feel average.

The design follows the food philosophy too — simple structure, strong details, and a little bit of drama where it matters.

Why quesabirria

Travana is a love letter to the taco that kept missing from the local scene.

The crispy shell, the cheese pull, the broth dip, the lime, the cilantro, the speed of the order — it all works when the execution is focused.

Focused menu

One signature item gives the brand a clean memory hook.

Event ready

Less complexity means faster service and better consistency.

Premium feel

The brand should look as craveable as the food.

The brand is simple because the mission is simple.
Make the taco people text their friends about. Then make the line move.
